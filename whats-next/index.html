What’s next

Accommodation First. More Travel Products Over Time.

Our initial focus is accommodation distribution, with an Asia-first direct supply strategy and connections to broader global inventory. Future opportunities may include activities and experiences, as well as selected ground transportation categories excluding flights.

Future capabilities are subject to development priorities, technical feasibility, and commercial availability. Statuses do not represent guaranteed launch commitments.

Future directions

Building Beyond Accommodation.

Accommodation is our starting point. As the KabuK Exchange ecosystem develops, we envision additional travel products and new ways for partners to access and distribute inventory.

Planned

White-Label Travel Solutions

We envision configurable, partner-branded travel experiences that help businesses bring accommodation and other travel products to customers under their own identity.

  • Partner-branded storefronts
  • Accommodation first
  • Other travel products over time

Storefronts powered by Exchange supply would be an Exchange offering; any reusable white-label technology underneath would belong to KabuK Platform.

Planned

Travel Agency Portal

A planned browser-based way for travel agencies to search accommodation inventory, review rates and availability, and manage reservations without building an API integration.

  • Search accommodation inventory
  • Review rates and availability
  • Manage reservations
Planned

Activities & Experiences

We aim to broaden the network over time to support tours, attractions and destination experiences alongside accommodation.

  • Tours
  • Attractions
  • Destination experiences
Exploring

Ground Transportation

We are evaluating opportunities involving airport transfers, private transport, rail and other non-flight ground transportation services.

  • Airport transfers
  • Private transport
  • Rail
  • Car rental

✕Flights are not part of this proposed scope.

Access models

More Ways to Access the Network.

API connectivity is one approach to working with a travel distribution network. Over time, we are also considering partner-branded experiences and a browser-based travel agency portal to support businesses with different technical capabilities. Availability and eligibility will depend on each product’s development status.

API connectivity

Direct system-to-system integration for partners with technical teams. Scope and availability are confirmed with each partner during onboarding.

Planned

White-Label Travel Solutions

Partner-branded experiences for businesses that prefer not to build their own booking front end.

Planned

Travel Agency Portal

A browser-based option for agencies that want to work with the network without an API integration.
