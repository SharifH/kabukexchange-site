Supply strategy

Local Depth in Asia. Global Breadth Through Connectivity.

Our approach combines a focus on direct accommodation relationships across Asia with connections to broader global inventory. That combination is designed to give distribution partners more ways to source accommodation through a single network.

01

Direct Property Connections

We prioritize direct relationships with hotels, ryokan, hotel groups and other accommodation providers across Asia.

02

Connected Global Inventory

Third-party wholesalers, bedbanks and other integrated suppliers can extend accommodation coverage beyond our direct network.

03

One Distribution Connection

KabuK Exchange is designed to coordinate content, availability, pricing, booking and settlement workflows across participating supply and demand partners.

Asia-first describes our supply development priority, not a limit on where partners distribute. Connected global inventory is sourced through integrated third-party suppliers, not direct contracts.

    Why KBKx

    Built for the Complexity of Travel Distribution.

    Travel distribution involves more than moving rates and availability between systems. KabuK Exchange is being designed to connect multiple sources of accommodation supply with diverse distribution channels while coordinating the commercial and operational workflows that sit between them.

    1. Regional Supply Focus

      We prioritize developing direct accommodation relationships across Asia, where local depth matters.

    2. Broader Inventory Connectivity

      Connected third-party suppliers complement direct supply, giving partners more ways to source accommodation.

    3. Unified Distribution Workflows

      Content, mapping, availability, pricing, booking and settlement are coordinated through one network relationship.

    4. Room to Expand

      The network is designed to extend over time to new access models and travel products beyond accommodation.

    What’s next

    Building Beyond Accommodation.

    Accommodation is our starting point. As the KabuK Exchange ecosystem develops, we envision additional travel products and new ways for partners to access and distribute inventory.

    Planned

    White-Label Travel Solutions

    We envision configurable, partner-branded travel experiences that help businesses bring accommodation and other travel products to customers under their own identity.

    Planned

    Travel Agency Portal

    A planned browser-based way for travel agencies to search accommodation inventory, review rates and availability, and manage reservations without building an API integration.

    Planned

    Activities & Experiences

    We aim to broaden the network over time to support tours, attractions and destination experiences alongside accommodation.

    Exploring

    Ground Transportation

    We are evaluating opportunities involving airport transfers, private transport, rail and other non-flight ground transportation services. Flights are not part of this proposed scope.

    Future capabilities are subject to development priorities, technical feasibility, and commercial availability. Statuses do not represent guaranteed launch commitments.
