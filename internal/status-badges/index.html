Connection-method badges

Via Technology Partner

Not allowed: method alone

A route without a status reads as availability. Always pair it with a verified status, or omit the listing.

Coming SoonVia Technology Partner

Not allowed without evidence

“Supported by a connectivity partner” is not “coming soon on KBKx”. Coming Soon needs owner-approved launch readiness.

  • Don’t treat a connection-method badge as production availability — it describes a route only.
  • Don’t show a status that KBKx has not verified for this provider, even if the provider is live elsewhere.
  • Don’t use more than one status or more than one method badge per listing.
  • Don’t publish a provider or its logo without publicDisclosureApproved and logoUseApproved.
  • Don’t name Bakuun on a listing without connectionProviderDisclosureApproved for that provider.

Do

  • Use one status badge per item (and at most one connection-method badge), from the catalog keys only.
  • Map products to statuses in data/product-roadmap.json, never in page markup.
  • Record owner approval (approved, approvedBy, approvedOn) before publishing a status.
  • Downgrade to Exploring, or hide the badge, when approval is uncertain.
  • Pair future capabilities with the roadmap disclaimer.

Don’t

  • Don’t use Coming Soon as a generic synonym for Planned.
  • Don’t pair Planned or Exploring with dates or delivery promises.
  • Don’t rely on colour alone, or restyle a badge locally.
  • Don’t mark anything Live without verified general availability.
  • Don’t put confidential roadmap details, commercial terms or secrets on this page.
