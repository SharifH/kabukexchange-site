How It Works

One operating flow across hotel distribution

KBKx coordinates the commercial, technical, operational, reservation, and financial workflows required to move a hotel partnership from initial alignment into reliable production operations.

KabuK Exchange is the distribution network. The technology described here is provided by KabuK Platform, a separate KabuK Style product with its own website. KabuK Platform

01

Connect

Establish the appropriate API, connectivity-provider, or supply relationship.

02

Map & validate

Align properties, rooms, rate plans, policies, and partner-specific business rules.

03

Distribute

Apply inventory, eligibility, pricing, margin, and channel rules across approved relationships.

04

Operate

Manage reservation lifecycle, exceptions, support, financial records, and partner visibility.

Data and transaction flows

Designed around the hotel distribution lifecycle

Supply flow

Property content, room structures, availability, pricing, policies, and sourcing data enter through approved supply relationships.

Distribution flow

Eligible inventory is exposed according to partner agreements, commercial rules, and channel requirements.

Reservation flow

Bookings move through confirmation, modification, cancellation, operational tracking, and financial recognition.
