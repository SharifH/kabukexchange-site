AI & Automation

Intelligence that strengthens travel operations.

KAOS—KabuK Assisted Operations System—is an AI-assisted capability in development for the KabuK Exchange network, designed to reduce repetitive work while keeping people, context, and accountability at the center.

KabuK Exchange is the distribution network. The technology described here is provided by KabuK Platform, a separate KabuK Style product with its own website. KabuK Platform

Assistance, not a black box

  • Human review for material operational decisions
  • Explainable recommendations and traceable actions
  • Shared context across onboarding, mapping, and partner operations
  • Designed as a platform layer—not a disconnected AI add-on
Planned capabilities

Support teams across the partner lifecycle

Assisted onboarding

Review submitted information, identify gaps, and help teams move properties and partners toward readiness.

Mapping assistance

Recommend property, room, and rate-plan matches while preserving human review for uncertain decisions.

Operational support

Help teams investigate issues, follow SOPs, prepare responses, and escalate cases with the right context.

Partner success

Surface performance changes, follow-up needs, and potential risks before they become larger operational problems.

Executive insight

Summarize meaningful operational, commercial, and financial developments for platform leadership.

Workflow automation

Reduce repetitive coordination while maintaining approvals, audit history, and accountable ownership.

Platform roadmap

Sell today's value. Build toward tomorrow's intelligence.

KAOS capabilities will be introduced progressively as workflows, controls, and partner data reach the maturity required for reliable operation. Availability will vary by capability and partner program.
