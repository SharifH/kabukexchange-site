Connection labels

Direct, Indirect and Planned Connectivity

We label how each connection is made separately from its current status, so a technical route is never mistaken for availability.

  • Direct Integration

    KabuK Exchange connects to the provider's system without an intermediary.

  • Via Technology Partner

    The connection is made through an established upstream connectivity partner, not a direct integration.

  • Planned

    On our roadmap and not yet available. Planned connections are never presented as live.

A connection method describes the technical route to a provider. It does not by itself indicate that the integration is live or available to all KabuK Exchange partners.
