## Brand hierarchy

- **Category:** Asia-first accommodation distribution network
- **Headline promise:** Direct Connections in Asia. Broader Inventory Worldwide.
- **Supply strategy line:** Local Depth in Asia. Global Breadth Through Connectivity.
- **Future line:** Building Beyond Accommodation. (Every future capability carries a catalog status badge and the roadmap disclaimer; flights are out of scope.)
- **Relationship:** KabuK Exchange is powered by KabuK Platform, the technology foundation. A product of KabuK Style Inc.

## Primary audiences

1. **Supply Partners:** hotels, hotel groups, wholesalers, OTAs acting as supply sources, other travel suppliers. Hotels keep a dedicated "Link Your Property" path.
2. **Demand Partners:** OTAs, travel agencies, DMCs, bedbanks, travel apps, membership platforms.
3. **Connectivity Partners:** PMS, channel managers, CRS platforms (integration rails).

## Messaging rules

1. Lead with distribution/network value, not with being "the platform".
2. Name concrete partner outcomes; avoid abstract "innovation" language.
3. Distinguish available capabilities from roadmap capabilities.
4. Never claim scale, reach, rates, SLAs or partners that are not verified.
5. Mention KabuK Platform as the foundation beneath KBKx, briefly.

The canonical runtime strings are maintained in `i18n/en.json` (then `python3 build.py`).
